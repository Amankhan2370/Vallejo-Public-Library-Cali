About Vallejo Public Library

Established in 1920, Vallejo Public Library has been a cornerstone of the community, offering a vast collection of books, digital resources, and a variety of programs for all ages.

Our Mission

To provide access to information, foster a love of reading, and support lifelong learning for the residents of Vallejo.
